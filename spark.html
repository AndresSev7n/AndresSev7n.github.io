SPARK: Career Interest App for Students

Middle schoolers explore career interests in English or Spanish, with questions built for their grade.

The Problem

Middle school students often have few structured, age-appropriate ways to explore career interests, and Spanish-speaking students need materials in their own language.

What I Built

SPARK is an educational career-interest app I designed and built. It is designed for teachers to use with their students to start conversations about interests and possible careers.

  • Grade 6: its own question bank, in English and Spanish
  • Grade 7: its own question bank, in English and Spanish
  • Grade 8: uses the O*NET Interest Profiler Short Form (English)

What this shows

Needs analysis, age-appropriate content design, bilingual accessibility, and a working prototype ready for classroom use.
