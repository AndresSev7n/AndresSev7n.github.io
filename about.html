About and Contact

I help people get comfortable with technology they have never used, under real pressure.

I'm a bilingual trainer and instructional designer. For about 6 years I taught dentists and clinical staff to use unfamiliar 3D imaging software, often mid-procedure. Today, as a Special Education Paraprofessional, I adapt lessons in real time for students with a range of learning needs.

Both roles taught the same lesson: a system built for one kind of user can lose someone else through pace, unclear steps, or assumed knowledge. Closing that gap is the work I do as an instructional designer.

My focus: educational technology, AI-supported learning, accessibility, and ethical use of student data. Technology should solve a real problem for the learner.
