Personalization means designing for the person in front of you, not the same activity in different flavors.

I design learning experiences with generative AI and VR to build accessible, differentiated resources, and I check that they work for real people.

Project 1: Personalized Learning Pathways

This project applies three learning theories, Cognitive Load Theory, Universal Design for Learning, and Self-Determination Theory, to design distinct learning pathways for three learners with different readiness levels and needs. Each design decision is grounded in the specific mechanism the theory predicts, not just the theory's name. The goal was to show that personalization means designing for the person in front of you, not offering the same activity in different flavors.

  • Theories applied: Cognitive Load Theory, Universal Design for Learning, Self-Determination Theory
  • Audience: Three learners with different readiness levels and needs

What this shows

Theory-grounded design decisions, differentiation for real learners, and responsible use of AI to build accessible resources.
